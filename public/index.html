About the school

Nurturing every child from bud to bloom

Rose Buds Public School was founded under Rose Buds Education Society, led by Mrs. Davinder Kaur and inspired by founding father Col. Satpal Singh. The school was built around a simple promise: guide young learners with discipline, care, and quality education so they grow into responsible citizens.

Mission

Empower every student to achieve academic excellence.

Vision

A nurturing ground for lifelong learners to explore, create, and thrive.
